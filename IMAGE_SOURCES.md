## How to Download

1. Visit the URLs above
2. Right-click and "Save Image As..." 
3. Save to the `public/games/` or `public/consoles/` folder
4. Use descriptive names like: `ps5-console.jpg`, `xbox-controller.jpg`, `fortnite.jpg`
